## What changed in play

The agenda offers visible network fronts, leading directly to a connection and contextual decision desk. Early maps fit their visible nodes instead of showing a tiny network inside an almost empty fixed viewbox. New dark green/charcoal workstation tokens preserve the existing root/scroll architecture.

Researchers and lawyers explicitly select a held record associated with the selected actor or either endpoint of the selected tie. The engine now honors this record for comparison, verification, publication and evidence-chain work, rather than silently taking an unrelated first eligible claim. Lawyers cannot chain a rumor by falling through to another record. Contextual repeat use is blocked for the same action/record or target within the turn; available decisions evolve with memory, live edge state, evidence and investigation heat.

Network/actor plans offer only available methods:
- Quiet contact improves secrecy at the cost of reach/dependency; trust response arrives two turns later.
- Institutional channel spends four influence, trades lower relation tension for dependency/exposure, and brings institutional attention next turn.
- Rapid intervention is Saha-only, requires an extra action plus four covert resources, extends reach and creates actor heat; later tension/investigation blowback follows.

Callbacks change visible relationships and institutional state and identify the originating turn. Actor actions retain existing memory propagation. New plans use existing schema-5 tags; saves retain `jitem-derin-ag-v3`, and legacy actions without the new contextual flag execute their prior path without extra RNG draws. Evidence certainty is not promoted to historical fact.

## Real limitations

Existing historical/event opening choices are preserved; this wave strengthens the network decision desk rather than replacing every event with a new interface. Source catalogue titles and provenance still retain existing Turkish source text in the English UI; the new interaction and callback copy have TR/EN equivalents. The three representative plays are not an end-to-end balance certification. Visual quality remains subject to user acceptance.
